Get your teeth taken care of

No dental plan at work. These are the doors that are actually open — for cooks, servers, dishwashers, bartenders, anyone else working without it.

You don't need insurance to get seen. You need the right number to call. The options below are real, local, and verified — sliding-scale means they charge based on what you earn, not what you don't have.

Start here

Piedmont Health — sliding scale, no insurance needed

Burlington Community Health Center dental clinic is a federally-funded health center that charges based on your income. If you're earning restaurant wages, your visit cost goes down accordingly — sometimes to a few dollars. They do exams, x-rays, cleanings, fillings, and extractions. Call and tell them you're uninsured and want to set up as a new dental patient.

Phone: (336) 506-5878 · Burlington, NC · M–F 8am–6pm (Fri until 5)

I don't have dental insurance. I want to set up as a new patient and ask about the sliding-scale fee.

Say exactly this. They know what to do from there.

What to bring:

  • Photo ID
  • Proof of income (pay stub, bank statement, or tax form)
  • Proof of Alamance County address (any mailed bill)
No income docs? Bring what you have and explain. FQHCs are federally required to serve everyone regardless of ability to pay or immigration status — they will work something out. Don't let paperwork be the reason you don't call.
01

Check if Medicaid covers you first

If your income is under around $1,800/month, there's a good chance dental care is free — no sliding scale, actual $0.

  1. NC expanded Medicaid in late 2023. Adults 19–64 earning up to 138% of the federal poverty level qualify — that's roughly $21,600/year. Tips, cash shifts, irregular income: use your real average, not your best week.
  2. What it covers for adults. Emergency and medically necessary dental, including extractions. A cracked tooth that's causing pain qualifies. It won't cover cleanings or non-emergency fillings for adults, but it covers the stuff that actually hurts.
  3. Apply in 15 minutes. Call NC FAST at 1-888-622-7328 or go to epass.nc.gov. Coverage can start the same month you apply. Piedmont Health (above) accepts Medicaid — so these two paths meet at the same clinic.
02

Other doors worth knowing

If Piedmont has a wait or you need something the sliding-scale clinic doesn't do — oral surgery, wisdom teeth, sedation — here's where to go next.

Open Door Clinic free · Alamance residents

Free clinic for uninsured adults in Alamance County earning at or below 250% of the poverty line. They run a dental referral program that covers two dental visits through Burlington Community Health Center at minimal copay. Good first stop if you want help navigating the system. (336) 570-9800 · 424 Rudd St, Suite 102, Burlington

UNC Carolina Dentistry ~25 min · oral surgery on-site

UNC Adams School of Dentistry in Chapel Hill. Faculty and student clinic with full oral surgery capability, including wisdom tooth extractions and sedation. Dental school pricing is typically 50–70% below what a private practice charges, and they accept Medicaid. If Piedmont can't handle a complex extraction, this is the referral destination. (919) 537-3737 · Chapel Hill

NC Dental Society MOM Clinics free events · first come, first served

Volunteer dentists show up, see as many patients as they can in a day, and charge nothing. Cleanings, fillings, extractions — real work done for free. Dates and locations change every year, so check the schedule: ncdental.org. Worth putting on your radar.
