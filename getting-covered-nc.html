Any walk-in clinic one and done

Urgent care and pharmacy clinics do cash-pay physicals and can draw blood. Costs more per visit and there's no relationship, but it works in a pinch. Call first and ask the cash price.

The insurance piece (no rush, but know the date)

None of the above needs insurance — that's the point. But for real coverage down the road, there's a calendar catch worth knowing now. NC Medicaid's out (you earn over the ~$1,800/month line), so the route is the ACA Marketplace at healthcare.gov — and you can't just buy a plan mid-year right now.

  1. The timing gate. Open enrollment closed January 15, 2026, and the old year-round low-income window got killed in 2025. Right now you can only buy a Marketplace plan if you've had a qualifying life event — losing other coverage, a move, getting married — and only for 60 days after. Got one? Jump on it: healthcare.gov, or a free NC Navigator at 1-855-733-3711.
  2. The real play: this fall. Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs about Nov 1, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027 at healthcare.gov. Whether you get help paying — and how much — comes down to your actual yearly income, so run your real number through the estimator when you sign up. The Navigator (1-855-733-3711) walks you through it free.
  3. A bridge, if you want one. Worried about a bad accident before fall? A short-term health plan is a cheap, fast stopgap — but it's not the real thing: it can skip pre-existing conditions and preventive care. A patch, not a plan.
